Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Binh AI Content Manager is a private application used by its owner to manage the owner's own content publishing workflow. This application uses YouTube API Services.

Google and YouTube data accessed

The application may access basic channel and video metadata, information necessary to verify the authorized channel, and non-monetary analytics such as views and watch-performance data. It also uses authorization credentials required to upload new videos to the owner's channel.

The application is designed to use only the scopes needed for YouTube account read-only access, YouTube video upload, and YouTube Analytics read-only access.

How data is used

Google and YouTube data is used only to verify the authorized channel, upload original videos, monitor content performance, and improve future content decisions for the same owner. The application is not offered as a public service and does not manage third-party channels.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold. The application does not share Google user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by infrastructure or service providers only as necessary to operate the private workflow.

Data storage and security

Authentication secrets and refresh tokens are stored in protected runtime secret storage and are not intentionally exposed in public source code. Access is limited to the application owner and systems necessary to operate the workflow.

Data retention

Operational metadata is retained only for as long as needed to operate, troubleshoot, or evaluate the private workflow. The application does not intentionally create a permanent archive of Google user data beyond what is necessary for these purposes.

Data deletion and revocation

The owner may revoke the application's Google Account access at any time by visiting Google Account Connections. After access is revoked, the application can no longer retrieve new Google or YouTube data for that account.

The owner may also delete application-maintained operational data and credentials from the private runtime systems used by the workflow. Questions or requests regarding deletion may be sent to binhhappy90@gmail.com.

Limited Use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party policies

Use of YouTube information is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent to binhhappy90@gmail.com.
