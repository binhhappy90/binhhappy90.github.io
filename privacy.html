Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Binh AI Content Manager is a private application used by its owner to manage the owner's own video channel workflow.

Data accessed

The application may access basic channel and video metadata, permission information necessary to verify the authorized channel, and non-monetary analytics such as views and watch-performance data. It may also use authorization credentials required to upload videos to the owner's channel.

How data is used

Data is used only to verify the authorized channel, upload original videos, monitor content performance, and improve future content decisions. The application is not offered as a public service and does not manage third-party channels.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold. The application does not share Google user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly as necessary to operate the private workflow.

Data storage and security

Authentication secrets and refresh tokens are stored in protected runtime secrets and are not intentionally exposed in public source code. Access is limited to the application owner and the systems required to operate the workflow.

Data retention and deletion

Operational metadata may be retained only as long as needed for the private workflow. The owner may revoke Google account access at any time through the Google Account permissions page. If access is revoked, the application can no longer access the corresponding Google data. Locally or privately stored application data can be deleted by the owner.

Limited Use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent to binhhappy90@gmail.com.
